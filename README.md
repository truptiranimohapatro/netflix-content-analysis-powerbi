## 🎯 Project Overview

This project analyzes Netflix movies and TV shows using Microsoft Power BI. The interactive dashboard helps explore content distribution, release-year trends, genres, and country-wise availability to understand patterns in Netflix's content library.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

- 🎛️ **Interactive filter panel:** Filter content using available dashboard slicers.
- 🎬 **Content type analysis:** Comparison of Movies vs. TV Shows.
- 📅 **Release year analysis:** Distribution of titles by release year.
- 🎭 **Genre analysis:** Explore Netflix content across different genres.
- 🌎 **Country-wise analysis:** Analyze content distribution by country.
- 🎥 **Director analysis:** Explore titles by director, where available.
- ⭐ **Rating analysis:** Understand content distribution by rating category.
- ⏱️ **Duration analysis:** Explore movie durations and TV show seasons.

## 🔍 Key Insights

**Overall**
- The dataset contains **8,807 Netflix titles**, including movies and TV shows.
- **Movies account for approximately 70%** of the content, while TV shows represent around 30%.

**Content Type**
- Netflix has a larger number of movies than TV shows.
- Movies make up the majority of the titles available in the dataset.

**Release Year**
- Content releases increased significantly during the 2010s.
- The dataset includes titles released across several decades.

**Genre Analysis**
- **International Movies** and **Dramas** are among the most represented categories.
- Comedies and documentaries also contribute to the variety of content.

**Country Analysis**
- The **United States** has the highest number of titles in the dataset.
- India is also a major contributor to Netflix's content library.

**Content Ratings**
- **TV-MA** and **TV-14** are among the most common content ratings.
- Netflix's catalog includes content intended for different audience groups.
